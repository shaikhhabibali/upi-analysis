# Key Findings

- Monthly UPI transaction volume expanded approximately 9.1× from April 2021 to September 2026.
- Monthly UPI transaction value expanded approximately 6.0× over the same period.
- Average ticket size changed from ₹1,869 to ₹1,220, representing a 34.7% decline.
- The latest available YoY transaction-volume growth in September 2026 was 22.6%.
- Average daily UPI activity was highest in March at a seasonality index of 121.5, and lowest in April at 82.1.
- October-November averaged 104.5 on the seasonality index versus 99.1 for other months.
- Banks live and transaction volume show a level correlation of 0.98; the monthly-change correlation is 0.19.
